Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

Diondi LLC owns and operates ClassFlow System, a software platform used by educational centers to manage students, attendance, scheduling, reporting, communication, and related center operations.

Information we collect

Depending on how ClassFlow is used, we may process names, email addresses, telephone numbers, student and guardian information, attendance records, schedules, account information, communications, and records of SMS consent where applicable.

How information is used

  • Provide, maintain, and secure ClassFlow System.
  • Send operational and account-related communications.
  • Send authorized attendance notices, scheduling updates, reminders, and educational-center communications.
  • Prevent fraud and protect the service.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

SMS and mobile information

Mobile information and SMS opt-in consent data will not be sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

We may disclose mobile information to service providers only as needed to deliver authorized text messages, operate ClassFlow System, maintain security, prevent fraud, or comply with law. Such service providers are not authorized to use SMS opt-in data for their own marketing.

Message frequency varies according to center activity and the notifications selected. Message and data rates may apply.

Service providers

We may use service providers for hosting, data storage, email delivery, SMS delivery, security, analytics, and related functions necessary to operate ClassFlow. These providers process information to provide their services.

Data retention

We retain information as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain business and security records, resolve disputes, and comply with applicable obligations.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information by contacting us. Certain information may need to be retained for legal, contractual, security, or operational reasons.

SMS opt-out

You may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for assistance. Opting out of SMS does not prevent necessary non-SMS communications through other channels.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to contact@diondi.org.
