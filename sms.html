SMS Messaging Disclosures

Information about text messages sent through ClassFlow System.

Program identity

ClassFlow System is a product owned and operated by Diondi LLC. Educational centers use ClassFlow to send authorized operational text messages to parents, guardians, students, staff, and other intended recipients.

Types of messages

  • Attendance and check-in or check-out notices.
  • Schedule changes and appointment reminders.
  • Student progress or center-operation updates.
  • Account, support, or service-related notices.

Consent

Recipients receive SMS messages only after providing affirmative consent to the educational center or through another documented and legally valid opt-in method. SMS consent is optional and is not a condition of purchasing a product or service.

Frequency and charges

Message frequency varies according to center activity and the notifications selected. Message and data rates may apply.

Stopping messages

Reply STOP to unsubscribe from SMS messages. After opting out, you may receive one final confirmation message.

Getting help

Reply HELP for assistance or contact contact@diondi.org.

Mobile-information privacy

Mobile information and SMS opt-in information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
